# AIAP Platform 8.2.0 — reissued package

This reissued package is the downloadable replacement for the unavailable v8.2.0 attachment.

- Communication manages Activities and Events only.
- Communication has no Finance, Members, Users/Roles, Audit Logs, or Announcements management.
- Super Admin retains full administrative control and content publishing.
- President manages Members and Announcements, but not Finance, Activities, or Events.
- Secretariat manages Members only among operational modules; no Finance, Activities, Events, or Announcements management.
- Treasurer manages Finance only among operational modules.
- Every authenticated role can read/update its own profile through `/api/me/profile`.
- Added `/api/uploads/video` for MP4/WebM/MOV activity video uploads.
